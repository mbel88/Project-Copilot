# Microsoft Copilot Project Submission
## Supply Chain Compliance and Risk Management

**Prepared by:** Marie Belin  
**Submission date:** 25 November 2026

## 1. Project Overview

This submission demonstrates the use of Microsoft Copilot across a multi-step supply chain compliance scenario. The project covers four tasks:

1. Identifying and summarizing critical supply chain and compliance issues.
2. Coordinating a cross-functional review meeting and developing an action plan.
3. Analyzing supply chain risk data and documenting mitigation recommendations.
4. Presenting the findings, risks, and recommended actions to stakeholders.

The submission includes the Copilot prompts used during the project, the resulting deliverables, the supporting source materials, and the documentation required to navigate and review the package.

## 3. How to Review the Submission

For the clearest review experience, follow this sequence:

1. Open **01_Prompt_Library/Library_of_Copilot_Prompts.docx** to review the prompts used for each task.
2. Review the deliverables in folders **02** through **05** in task order.
3. Refer to **06_Supporting_Files** when source evidence or additional context is required.
4. In **Expanded_SupplyChainMetrics.xlsx**, review both the **Dataset** and **Analysis** worksheets.
5. In **Supply Chain Issue Compliance Report.pptx**, review the slides together with the associated speaker notes.
